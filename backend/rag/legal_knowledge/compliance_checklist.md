# MSME Statutory & Regulatory Compliance Checklist

## Key Compliance Categories by Business Type
1. **General Business Licensing**:
   - Udyam Registration (MSME Certificate)
   - Shops & Establishments Registration (State specific)
   - Trade License / Factory License
   - GST Registration (Mandatory if turnover > Rs. 20 Lakhs for services / Rs. 40 Lakhs for goods)

2. **Industry-Specific Licenses**:
   - FSSAI License (Food & Beverage)
   - Pollution Control Board NOC (Air & Water - Manufacturing/Chemicals)
   - Import Export Code (IEC) (For cross-border trade)
   - Drug License (Pharma/Healthcare)

3. **Periodic Filings & Obligations**:
   - Monthly/Quarterly GST Returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B)
   - Quarterly TDS Returns (Form 24Q, Form 26Q)
   - Monthly EPF & ESI Challans (By 15th of following month)
   - Annual Income Tax Return (ITR) & Tax Audit (Form 3CD if applicable)
   - Annual ROC Filings (AOC-4, MGT-7 for Companies/LLPs)
