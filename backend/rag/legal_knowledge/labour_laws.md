# Indian Labour Laws & Employment Compliance for MSMEs

## Shops and Establishment Act / Factories Act
1. Working hours restricted to 8-9 hours per day (48 hours per week max). Overtime payable at 2x normal rate.
2. Mandatory leave policies (Earned Leave, Casual Leave, Sick Leave).
3. Employment contracts must clearly state notice period (typically 30 days or payment in lieu).

## EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) & ESI (Employees' State Insurance)
1. EPF mandatory for establishments with 20 or more employees (applicable for employees drawing basic salary up to Rs. 15,000/month).
2. ESI mandatory for establishments with 10 or more employees (drawing gross salary up to Rs. 21,000/month).
3. Non-remittance of employer/employee EPF/ESI contributions attracts prosecution and heavy damages under Sections 14B & 7Q of EPF Act.

## Non-Compete & IP Assignment in Employment Agreements
1. Post-employment non-compete covenants restricting employment in competitor firms are unenforceable under Section 27 of Indian Contract Act.
2. Non-solicitation of clients/employees and strict confidentiality obligations are enforceable if reasonable in scope and duration (e.g. 1-2 years max).
3. Inventions & Works created during employment belong to employer provided IP Assignment clause is explicitly drafted.
