# Goods and Services Tax (GST) Compliance & Rules for MSMEs

## Invoicing & Input Tax Credit (ITC) Rules
1. Tax Invoices must include GSTIN of Supplier, GSTIN of Recipient, HSN/SAC Code, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, and Place of Supply.
2. Input Tax Credit (ITC) can only be claimed under Section 16(2) of CGST Act if:
   - Recipient possesses tax invoice/debit note.
   - Goods/services have been received.
   - Tax charged has been actually paid to the Government by supplier in GSTR-3B.
   - Recipient files GSTR-3B return.
3. 180-Day Rule (Proviso to Sec 16(2)): If recipient fails to pay supplier value of supply + tax within 180 days from invoice date, an amount equal to ITC availed must be added to output tax liability along with 18% per annum interest.

## Key Deadlines & Filings
- GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies): 11th of every month (Monthly filers) or 13th following quarter (QRMP).
- GSTR-3B (Summary Return): 20th of every month (Monthly filers).
- Late fees: Rs. 50/day (Rs. 20/day for Nil return) up to maximum prescribed caps.

## Contractual Safeguards for GST
1. Indemnity Clause for ITC Loss: Contracts should explicitly state that if supplier fails to upload GSTR-1 or pay tax causing recipient ITC loss, supplier must indemnify recipient for ITC lost + interest + penalty.
2. Payment withholding on non-reflection in GSTR-2B: Payment may be linked to tax reflection in recipient's GSTR-2B portal.
